How renting works

Questions people ask

How long can I rent a saree or lehenga for?

A standard rental covers the day before your event for collection, the event day, and the day after for return. Longer bookings are available on request.

Is cleaning included?

Yes. Every piece is professionally cleaned between rentals and you return it as worn. Cleaning is part of the rental.

Can I try on before I book?

Yes. Walk in during store hours or message us for a fitting slot. Fittings are free and there is no obligation to book.

What if the blouse or lehenga does not fit?

We adjust at the fitting. Sarees drape to any size, and blouses and lehenga waists are pinned or altered to you before collection.
